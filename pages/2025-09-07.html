Dearest Abby,

Happy Sunday! “How could a Sunday be happy?”, you ask? Well, maybe HAPPY is a lofty goal… but I wanted to help make them just a little less scary.

Welcome to Sunday Unscaries, Maddie’s first ever web app (pardon the dust). It’s basically a halfway-iOS-app that I can update easily remotely without paying Apple jillions of dollars…

How does it work? Every Sunday, you’ll be pushed an “unscary” to help you get through the day. They come in many shapes and sizes - some may just be me babbling like this, others will involve getting something from that big box I gave you…point is, they’re surprises, and I hope they make your Sundays a little more bearable 🧸...because life is so much more bearable with you in it.

Lets go practice! For your first week, open the package with the pink lock. The code is the date of your wedding.
